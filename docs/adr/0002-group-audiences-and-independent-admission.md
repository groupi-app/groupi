# Group audiences preserve independent Event admission and participation

Formal Groups provide dynamic Event audience eligibility while Events retain
independent authority and participation. A newly eligible Group member can
discover shared upcoming Events; departure or required-onboarding loss removes
only that Group's grant. Other qualifying audiences, explicit Event invitations
and accepted Event membership retain their own authority. Group managers receive
no Event-management role through sharing.

Visibility determines who may discover or read an Event. Separate admission
policy controls direct joining, invitation or application; application submission
and approval both require current audience eligibility. Admission creates Attendee
membership with a Pending RSVP. A post-admission joining questionnaire belongs to
the Group's onboarding, independently of admission approval and ordinary tools.

The [approved Groups design](https://github.com/groupi-app/groupi/issues/261)
chose these boundaries over Group-owned Events, participation tied to continued
Group membership, and applications implemented as optional add-ons. Those
alternatives would let a community manager revoke independent Event commitments
or make audience/tool changes determine participation. Whole-Group audience
grants remain distinct from private Invite Lists, which copy people into ordinary
invitations without a continuing audience link.
